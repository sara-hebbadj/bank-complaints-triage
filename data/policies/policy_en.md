# Gulf Horizon Bank: Complaints and Card Disputes Policy (fictional)

*Gulf Horizon Bank is a fictional bank invented for a portfolio project. The service targets in this policy
are invented. Only the Sanadak 15-day rule in clause 10 describes a real process.*

## 1. Scope
1.1 This policy covers complaints, card disputes and fraud reports received in Arabic, English or French
through the app, e-mail, phone or a branch.
1.2 A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction about something the bank did or failed to do, where the
customer expects a fix, a refund or an answer.

## 2. How we record a complaint
2.1 Every complaint gets a reference number and is logged the day it arrives.
2.2 A message received on a weekend or public holiday is treated as received on the next business day for
our business-day targets. The 15 calendar days in clause 10 always count from the day of receipt.

## 3. Acknowledgement
3.1 We acknowledge every complaint in writing within 2 business days.
3.2 The acknowledgement states the reference number, the reason code, the policy clause, and the date after
which the customer may contact Sanadak.

## 4. Investigation and escalation
4.1 The team that owns the subject investigates the complaint.
4.2 A complaint still open after 5 business days is escalated to the Complaints Manager.
4.3 We aim to send a final written reply before the date in clause 10.

## 5. Card disputes
5.1 A card dispute is a request to reverse or correct a specific card or ATM transaction. Every card dispute is
also treated as a complaint.
5.2 Duplicate charge: the same payment was taken more than once. We ask the merchant's bank to reverse the
duplicate.
5.3 Wrong amount: the card was charged a different amount from the one the customer agreed to pay.
5.4 Goods or service not received, or not as described: the customer paid and the merchant did not deliver, or
delivered something broken or different.
5.5 Refund not received: the merchant promised a refund or the release of a deposit, and it has not reached
the card.
5.6 Recurring payment after cancellation: the customer cancelled a subscription and was charged again.
5.7 ATM: the machine did not dispense the amount debited, or a withdrawal is stuck.
5.8 Disputes are accepted up to 120 days after the transaction date. The customer may be asked for receipts
or messages from the merchant, never for a PIN, OTP, CVV or password.
5.9 A provisional credit, if any, is decided by a person after the investigation starts. An acknowledgement
never promises a refund.

## 6. Fraud and security
6.1 Lost, stolen or compromised card, phone or account: the card is blocked at once and the case goes to the
Fraud and Security team.
6.2 A transaction the customer does not recognise is treated as possible fraud: the card is blocked and the
Fraud and Security team investigates before any dispute with the merchant.
6.3 Phishing, scam calls and fake messages are reported to the Fraud and Security team. The bank will never
ask for a PIN, OTP, CVV, password or full card number by phone, SMS, e-mail or chat.

## 7. Fees and charges
7.1 A complaint about a fee, a charge or an exchange rate is reviewed against the published tariff. A fee
charged in error is refunded after review by a person.

## 8. Service complaints
8.1 Transfers, top-ups and direct debits that failed, are delayed or did not reach the recipient.
8.2 Delays in delivering or replacing a card.
8.3 Service quality: the conduct of staff, branches, the call centre or the app.
8.4 Account access, identity checks (KYC) and requests to close an account that were not handled.

## 9. Customers in vulnerable circumstances
9.1 A customer who mentions bereavement, serious illness, job loss or financial hardship, disability,
cognitive decline or a relationship breakdown is handled by a person in Customer Care, with priority.

## 10. Taking a complaint to Sanadak
10.1 If 15 calendar days have passed since the complaint was submitted and the customer has had no written
reply, or is not satisfied with the reply, the customer may take the complaint to Sanadak, the UAE financial
ombudsman (sanadak.gov.ae).

## 11. Records and personal data
11.1 The audit log keeps a hash of each message, never the message text, full card numbers or one-time codes.
11.2 Every decision records the model and prompt version used, the reason code, the policy clause and the
human decision, with any override reason.
